They also have prehensile upper lips, like elephants' trunks, to grasp and pull food into their mouths. Interestingly, manatees have to return to a freshwater river to drink while they enjoy swimming and grazing on seagrass in water.

In order to survive in tough and challenging conditions, manatees make a large quantity of special adaptations for breath. For example, they normally return to the {23:surface} of the water about every two to five minutes but stay submerged for as long as 20 minutes if necessary. Manatees' lungs are quite long. The lungs extend horizontally along their backs. With each breath, manatees exchange 90 percent of the air in their lungs, while humans exchange 10 percent when they breathe.

Manatees' movements look {24:elegant} just like ballet dancers. Thus they were easily {25:mistaken} for mermaids for ages. In spite of their graceful {26:characteristic}, however, manatees have a very impolite way of sinking and swimming in the sea, making farts. Manatees use their farts to swim. An adult manatee will eat between 100 and 150 pounds of {27:vegetation} each day, which means a lot of methane builds up inside.

Manatees are an {28:endangered} species. This is mainly due to their large size and slow movement in water that makes them {29:vulnerable} to hunters who attempt to collect their bones and oil. The number of manatees has {30:declined} for the sake of hunting pressure. Despite law protections, threats are everywhere. The gentle beasts are often hit by motorboats, and sometimes become entangled in fishing nets. It is our duty to protect this beautiful creature with care.

**干擾選項分析**：

**解題策略**：
1. **先判斷詞性**：根據文法結構排除詞性不符的選項

3. **小心形態相似的詞**：elegant/elegance, appearance/apparent, declined/decline
4. **理解上下文**：不只看空格附近，要理解整句甚至整段的意思

🧠 整體詳解

**干擾選項設計原理**：
1. **(K) advance** vs **(B) advancement**
   - 詞根相同：advanc-
   - 詞性不同：動詞/名詞（原形）vs 名詞（抽象）
   - 用法差異：advance 較少用作可數名詞，advancement 是標準的抽象名詞

2. **(L) efficient** vs **(E) efficiency**
   - 詞根相同：effici-
   - 詞性不同：形容詞 vs 名詞
   - 文法陷阱：「the ___ of」需要名詞，不能用形容詞

3. **(M) conversion** vs **(D) convert**
   - 詞根相同：convert/convers-
   - 詞性不同：名詞 vs 動詞
   - 文法陷阱：「can ___」需要動詞原形，不能用名詞

**解題技巧總結**：
1. **詞性判斷最關鍵**：先確定空格需要什麼詞性
2. **小心詞根相同的詞**：同一個詞根可能有多種詞性變化
3. **注意固定搭配**：accessible to, transition from...to, convert...into
4. **理解語境**：不只看文法，也要理解文章內容的邏輯
